# Scale-set JIT Secret outlives a worker reaped before its job completes

Since Q1151, a scale-set `JobCompleted` reclaims the Secret mounted by the worker whose `actions-gateway.com/runner-name` matches the runner the completion names.
If that worker is already gone when the completion arrives, the Secret is read off nothing.
The fallback deletes the Secret named for the completed job only when the worker minted for that job is gone too.
So when a job ran on another job's worker, that worker was deleted first, and the minted worker is still present, the credential-bearing JIT-config Secret the deleted worker mounted stays until the RunnerSet is deleted (Q373's leak, narrowed).

The window is small: a completion normally arrives seconds after the runner exits, and `completedPodTTL` holds a terminal pod for minutes.
It opens when a completion is delayed past that TTL (a listener down, or a queue replayed after restart) or when an operator deletes the pod by hand.
Found by the reviewer of the Q1151 fix, from source; the rate is unmeasured.

This is separate from Q1152 (which run recovery names) and Q1153 (an idle worker not reclaimed); none of the three fixes closes another.

**A likely fix** is to reclaim a worker's Secret when the reaper deletes the worker, reading the name off its `job-payload` volume, so the Secret's lifetime follows the pod rather than a message that may arrive after it.
