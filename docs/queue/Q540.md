# Milestone: Kata + Dragonfly at the node, pull-through cache in-guest

Validate the composed stack: node-level P2P for pod images, in-guest mirror for job pulls.
Nothing node-layer is built: `deploy/dragonfly/` holds only Q539's pod-network seed peer.

The worker half of the reachability question is answered ([Q539 plan §8.1](../plan/q539-dragonfly-mirror-backend.md#81-measured-the-worker-policy-holds-and-nothing-else-does)): on dogfood, a Kata worker under the GMC's workload policy times out on port 4001 at every node address, including its own node's and the `CiliumInternalIP`, while an unlabelled Kata pod beside it reaches all of them.
That was measured with a stand-in listener, not dfdaemon.

The open part is the rest of the cluster.
Chart 1.8.5's node client listens on `0.0.0.0:4001` with `hostNetwork`, Q539 measured that proxy as an open forward proxy that forwards pushes, and every pod with no egress policy reaches a node address on that port.
So the build has to keep the proxy off the pod network, for example by binding it to loopback for the node's own containerd; whether chart 1.8.5 can do that is unmeasured.
One deployment for both layers?
