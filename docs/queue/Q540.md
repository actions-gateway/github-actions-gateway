# Milestone: Kata + Dragonfly at the node, pull-through cache in-guest

Validate the composed stack: node-level P2P for pod images, in-guest mirror for job pulls.
Nothing node-layer is built: `deploy/dragonfly/` holds only Q539's pod-network seed peer.

The worker half of the reachability question is answered ([Q539 plan §8.1](../plan/q539-dragonfly-mirror-backend.md#81-measured-the-worker-policy-holds-and-nothing-else-does)): on dogfood, a Kata worker under the GMC's workload policy times out on port 4001 at every node address, including its own node's and the `CiliumInternalIP`, while an unlabelled Kata pod beside it reaches all of them.
That was measured with a stand-in listener, not dfdaemon.

The open part is the rest of the cluster.
Chart 1.8.5's node client runs with `hostNetwork`, and every pod with no egress policy reaches a node address.
Binding the proxy to loopback works on kind ([§8.2](../plan/q539-dragonfly-mirror-backend.md#82-measured-the-proxy-binds-to-loopback-and-five-ports-stay-on-every-address)): the chart sets it from values, containerd pulls through it, and peer transfer still works.
It leaves upload gRPC, metrics, health, stats and piece storage on `0.0.0.0`, and the upload service carries task download and delete calls with no authentication (read from source, not probed).
So the build first needs a design for restricting those ports, for example mutual TLS between peers, before anything reaches dogfood's Kata nodes; whether GKE's containerd reads `certs.d` per pull, as kind's does, is also unmeasured.
One deployment for both layers?
