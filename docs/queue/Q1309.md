# Date or withdraw the undated competitor claims in appendix D.1 to D.4

Appendix D dates every third-party claim from D.9 on ("Every claim about a third-party system is dated, because they move"), and `why-gag.md` stamps every ARC cell.
D.1 to D.4 predate both rules and still carry competitor claims with no version and no date.
Read on 2026-11-07, three were wrong or unsupported: D.1 said GitHub-hosted runners offer no egress-IP control and no GPUs, while GitHub's larger-runner docs list static IP ranges, GPU-powered runners and Azure private networking; D.3 claimed a 30 to 60 second scale-down lag for ARC that nothing measured and that scale-set mode does not have; D.3 cited the classic tier's ~12.2 KiB where the shipped scale-set tier measures ~7.7 KiB.
Those three were corrected in the positioning change that found them.

Done when every competitor-side sentence in D.1 to D.4 names the version and date it was read at, or is marked unverified in the same clause, or is gone, and the GAG-side node-placement claim names the test that pins it or is softened to what the chart does.
