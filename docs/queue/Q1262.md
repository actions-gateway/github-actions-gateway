# Retype the GMC onto api/v2 ahead of the v2alpha1 and v2beta1 drop

Blocked on Q273, the `v1alpha1` removal now in flight, which edits many of the same files: of the 34 files importing both `v1alpha1` and an older `v2` version that survive its deletions, 21 are the GMC's.
The first of three PRs that let [Q1167](Q1167.md) delete `api/v2alpha1` and `api/v2beta1` ([plan](../plan/q1167-v2-retype-split.md)); it runs in parallel with the classic removal (Q264).

The swap is mechanical: `api/v2` exports every `api/v2alpha1` symbol except the classic-protocol constants and the two FQDN alias constants, measured 2026-11-02.
Off `7b292627a`, 32 GMC non-test files and 77 tests import `v2alpha1` or `v2beta1` once Q273's two PRs (its test port onto `api/v2`, then the removal) are subtracted ([what that assumes](../plan/q1167-v2-retype-split.md#the-tree-this-plan-assumes-after-q273)).
What survives of `gag-migrate` is in scope, apart from `storageversion.go`, which reads `v2beta1` on purpose and is Q1167's.

Three sites are not mechanical:

- `egressproxy_fqdn.go`'s `CiliumFQDN` and `CalicoFQDN` arms are deleted, since no cluster reaches `v2.0.0` holding an alias ([why](../plan/q1167-v2-retype-split.md#why-a-retype-is-mechanical-everywhere-except-two-places)).
- `cmd/gmc/cmd/config.go`'s cache scoping for `PriorityClassAllowlist` retypes in the same change as its controller, or the controller's informer runs unscoped.
- `scalesetscope.go` reads `spec.acquisitionProtocol`, which `v2` lacks; it and `webhook/v2/runnerset_read.go` retype here if the classic removal has merged, else they stay on `v2alpha1` for Q1167.

Conversion tests stay as they are; Q1167 deletes them.
`v2alpha1` and `v2beta1` stay registered in the GMC's scheme, which the conversion webhook still needs until Q1167.
