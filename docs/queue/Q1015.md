# DirectEgress anti-vacuity control fails on calico, negative disqualified

**Event:** `E2E_V2_DirectEgress_MetadataServerBlocked` fails on `main` with `GATE_RC≠0` on either gated probe. → top of Queue, escalated.

`E2E_V2_DirectEgress_MetadataServerBlocked` failed on `main` once, in [e2e-calico merge_group run 33080749313](https://github.com/actions-gateway/github-actions-gateway/actions/runs/33080749313), 2026-08-27 14:19 UTC, at the anti-vacuity assertion, line 277 of the spec at that commit.
It is the only `e2e-calico` failure on `main` besides [Q549](Q549.md)'s in 317 runs since 2026-08-18 (270 `merge_group` and 47 `push`, counted 2026-09-07).

**The security assertion did not fail; its control did, and the control's pod did run.** The row as filed read the failure as the 2-minute pod wait; the log says otherwise.
The 30 s line in the output is Ginkgo's progress report, and the assertion fired at 14:19:47 on a pod that had run 14:19:32 to 14:19:42 and reported `CURL_RC=28`: a 10 s connect-timeout, packets dropped rather than refused.
The stand-in DaemonSet was 2/2 Ready on that node, and the unlabelled control on the same node had reached :80, taking 4 s for a curl to a local listener that should take milliseconds.

**Mechanism, measured 2026-09-07 on a local kind cluster with Calico v3.31.5.** Calico programs a new pod's endpoint after the container can already run, and everything the pod sends before that is dropped, the same per-endpoint window [Q221](../development/flake-watch-retired.md) measured for a selector peer.
With the workload policy applied and the stand-in listening, 15 of 15 labelled probes on a quiet cluster reached `169.254.169.254:53` on their first attempt in ≤0.01 s, so the allowance is in effect, not absent.
Under CPU pressure and pod, Service and NetworkPolicy churn on the same node, 17 of 17 probes that were admitted still reached it, but two single connects took 1.05 s and 1.77 s: the first SYN dropped and the 1 s retransmit accepted.
Three more trials were rejected by the kubelet with `OutOfpods` before any packet, a harness overload rather than a dataplane reading.
The runner's tail is longer than a Mac's: a passing run's unlabelled control waited over 12 s, and the failing pod's window outlasted the 10 s timeout.
This spec was the one shape with no retry across that window, because its destination is an IP literal and `runEgressProbe` retries only a failed resolve; every other probe in the suite targets a hostname.

**Mitigation shipped.** The control and the workload pod each gate on their allowed port with a bounded 150 s budget (`runGatedEgressProbe`), and the negative now runs in the same pod as the anti-vacuity gate, so a blocked :80 is asserted only from an endpoint proven programmed.
Inverted locally by removing the link-local peer from the policy: the exact script exhausted its budget with `GATE_RC=28 GATE_ATTEMPTS=30` in 161 s, so the gate cannot pass vacuously.

Residual: the window's CI tail is unmeasured (local maximum 1.77 s against a 10 s timeout it exceeded on the runner), so a recurrence past 150 s would say the mechanism is something else.
Soak clock runs from the fix; blast radius is the `e2e-calico` lane, which is merge-group-only ([Q675](../development/testing.md#path-gated-workflows-verify-the-heavy-gates-actually-ran)), bar ≥50 as a correctness-guarding spec.
[Q402](Q402.md) holds the related upstream finding that Google documents DPv2 as exempt from the extra NodeLocal DNSCache rule while Q229 measured the opposite.
