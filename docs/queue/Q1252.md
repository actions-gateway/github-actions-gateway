# Flake: lease-test's another-holder renewer loses its lock and survives

**Event:** `lease-test` fails `a renewer that finds another holder TERMs its gate` on a tree that changes nothing under `scripts/dogfood/`.

Observed 2026-11-02 at 01:04Z in CI `scripts-test` (job 125038110299, run 41369763048) on a GitHub-hosted `ubuntu-26.04` runner, on PR 2212's head `ae1082f`, whose diff touches only the gmc e2e suite, the `Makefile` e2e target and docs.
The same suite passed in a local `make check` of that tree on macOS.
Three lines failed together:

```
[lease-test] FAIL a renewer that finds another holder TERMs its gate: want '143', got '0'
[lease-test] FAIL the renewer says why it stopped the gate: 'another holder took' not in 'rmdir: failed to remove '/tmp/tmp.wCwpjk66D2/lease/fake-dogfood-proj-us-east1-b-gag-dogfood.lease.lock': No such file or directory
survived'
[lease-test] FAIL the stopped gate leaves the new holder's record: want 'ci-runner-7/4242', got 'owner-host/65490'
```

**Mechanism, unverified.** `lease_fake_locked` breaks the lock after 500 failed `mkdir` tries 10 ms apart, which assumes a lock held for 5 s belongs to a renewer killed mid-write.
A renewer that is merely starved inside its locked write would have its lock broken by the test's `remote_lease` write.
Its own `rmdir` would then fail under `set -e`, so the renewer dies without TERMing its gate, the gate sleeps out its 10 s and prints `survived`, and the renewer's write lands over the test's.
That chain fits all three lines, but nothing here measured the renewer's hold time on the runner or reproduced it.

This is a different case and a different mechanism from Q1202, which is the lost-lease renewer racing the owner's sleep.
It is also not the zombie `kill -0` failure that PR 2207 (Q1205) fixes, which happens on GAG runners and not GitHub-hosted ones.
