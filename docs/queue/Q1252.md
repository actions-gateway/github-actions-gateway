# Flake: lease-test's another-holder renewer loses its lock and survives

**Event:** `lease-test` fails `a renewer that finds another holder TERMs its gate`, or `a waiter does not break a live holder's lock`, on `main`.

Observed 2026-11-02 at 01:04Z in CI `scripts-test` (job 125038110299, run 41369763048) on a GitHub-hosted `ubuntu-26.04` runner, on PR 2212's head `ae1082f`, whose diff touches nothing under `scripts/dogfood/`.
Three lines failed together:

```
[lease-test] FAIL a renewer that finds another holder TERMs its gate: want '143', got '0'
[lease-test] FAIL the renewer says why it stopped the gate: 'another holder took' not in 'rmdir: failed to remove '/tmp/tmp.wCwpjk66D2/lease/fake-dogfood-proj-us-east1-b-gag-dogfood.lease.lock': No such file or directory
survived'
[lease-test] FAIL the stopped gate leaves the new holder's record: want 'ci-runner-7/4242', got 'owner-host/65490'
```

**Mechanism, reproduced 2026-11-01.** `lease_fake_locked` broke any lock held past 500 failed `mkdir` tries 10 ms apart, which takes at least 5 s and took 15 s at load 68 on the 18-core dev host.
A renewer starved inside its locked write therefore had its lock broken by the test's `remote_lease`.
The renewer runs `set +e`, so its failed `rmdir` did not stop it: its patch reported `renewed`, its write landed over the test's record, and the gate slept out its 10 s and printed `survived`.
Stalling the renewer's patch 45 s inside the lock, with the owner sleeping 60 s, printed all three lines above on the old lock.

**The fix makes the lock a symlink naming its holder's pid**, broken only once that pid has exited, zombies included.
The same stalled-renewer harness passes on it, and lease-test's new live-holder case fails on the old lock.
Both lock cases wait 10 s, so they bracket the old timed break's return: a break finishing inside 10 s fails the live-holder case, and one taking longer fails the killed-holder case, as it did at load 80 on the dev host.

This is a different case and a different mechanism from Q1202, which is the lost-lease renewer racing the owner's sleep.
It is also not the zombie `kill -0` failure that PR 2207 (Q1205) fixed, which happens on GAG runners and not GitHub-hosted ones.
