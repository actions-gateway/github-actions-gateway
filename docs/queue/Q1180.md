# Dogfood keeps no AGC or proxy metrics past the pod's life

Google Managed Prometheus is enabled on the dogfood cluster, but its only scrape targets are `kube-state-metrics` and `dcgm`: no `PodMonitoring` covers the AGC or the proxy, so no `actions_gateway_*` series has ever been stored (read-only survey, 2026-10-10).
Every retry counter (`eviction_retries_total{cause}`, `eviction_rerun_failures_total`, `scaleset_jobs_completed_total{result}`, the abandoned and startup-verdict counters) dies with the pod, and Cloud Logging's `_Default` bucket keeps only warn lines, for 30 days.
That leaves Q555 and Q1179 nothing to measure history against.
Add a `PodMonitoring` for the AGC and proxy in the dogfood tenant, using the mTLS client Secret the runbook already uses to sample `/metrics` by hand.
GMP's retention is 24 months by default, which is recalled and not checked here.
