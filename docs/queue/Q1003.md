# A lint red reports a sample of itself, not its full extent

`.golangci.yml` declares no `issues:` block, so golangci-lint's own defaults apply: `max-same-issues: 3` and `max-issues-per-linter: 50`.
Both truncate the report, and nothing in the output says so.

Measured 2026-08-27 by silencing `ST1001` in `linters.settings.staticcheck.checks` and linting `cmd/gmc/test/utils`, which holds five identical `//nolint:revive,staticcheck` dot-import directives.
The repo's config printed three and summarised them as `nolintlint: 3`; adding `max-same-issues: 0` printed all five as `nolintlint: 5`.
The two dropped reds are indistinguishable from not existing.
So a developer fixes the three the log names, pushes, and CI comes back with the next two: one wasted cycle per class that runs over three.

Fix: add an `issues:` block setting both caps to `0`, with a comment saying a gate reports its full extent rather than a sample.
The caps trade completeness for a readable console; this gate's reader is a CI log nobody scrolls twice, so completeness is the half worth keeping.

Found working Q929, where a truncated red is the difference between diagnosing a flake from its one log and not getting another chance.
This is a reporting defect only: `MaxSameIssues` and `MaxFromLinter` are registered *after* `NewNolintFilter` in `pkg/lint/runner.go` (2.12.2), so no cap can make a directive read unused.
