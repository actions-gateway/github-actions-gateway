# A failed job's recorded context is its still-Running pod (M4 measured)

Flaky-job detection records a failed job's platform context from the worker snapshot taken at the pod's terminal transition, falling back to reading the pod; measured on dogfood on 2026-11-22, the completion of a job that ends on a step's result arrives before that transition, so the record is the fallback's read of a pod still `Running`.
Every such record in the runs read had `podPhase: Running`: jobs `82a2530d…`, `abe6cad3…` and `ee99fae0…` of run 45052015868, and `44325908424/scripts-test` from 2026-11-18 ([the readings](../plan/q555-automatic-recovery-design.md#readings-from-the-dogfood-turn-up-2026-11-22)).
In the first two no container had terminated; in `ee99fae0…` the runner had just terminated `Completed`, exit 0, with the pod not yet terminal.
The listener logged each completion in the same second as `job started`, and the pods were reaped 5 minutes later (`completed_ttl`) in phase `Succeeded`.

Two consequences, both from `cmd/agc/internal/provisioner/flaky_jobs.go` read against those timings rather than observed directly:

- A fault that shows only on the terminated pod, such as a sidecar's termination reason or exit code, is never in the record, and no `PlatformFaultOnFailedJob` Warning names it.
  Faults visible on a running pod (a restart count, a `DisruptionTarget` condition, the colocated proxy's readiness) still land.
- `SnapshotScaleSetWorker` still stores a snapshot at the terminal transition, after the completion that would take it, so every worker leaves one in `workerSnapshots` until the 1,024-entry bound evicts the oldest, and each `put` past the bound scans the whole map.

The order inverts for a kill that ends the runner: an out-of-memory worker's completion arrived 9 min 48 s after its pod failed, and took the terminal snapshot, which is the case the out-of-memory class relies on.
The fix is a design choice: wait briefly for the terminal transition before recording, record at the transition and amend on completion, or stop snapshotting a pod whose job already completed.
The retry classes that read the snapshot, Q1315 and Q1316, should be weighed against it before they are built.
