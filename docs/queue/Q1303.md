# features.md badges v2 features beta while v2 is the GA version

`docs/features.md` carries 15 `gag-maturity-badge">beta` badges (`git grep`, 2026-11-06), including the one on "The v2 API" itself at `:123`, and [website.md](../development/website.md) defines that badge as "the API shape is still under its first stability contract".
`docs/getting-started.md:7` says `v2` is the General Availability version and the only one served, so at `v2.0.0` the two pages disagree in front of the same reader.

Q1107 left the badges because whether any feature keeps one past GA is a judgement per bullet, not a sweep: a field can still be young on a GA API.

**Done when** each of the 15 is removed or its bullet says why it stays, and `website.md` says what the badge means once `v2` is GA.
