# Gateway teardown waits on a finished worker pod until its node is gone

**Deleting an ActionsGateway within `completedPodTTL` of its last job holds the finalizer for a worker pod that already finished, until something other than the AGC deletes that pod.** On the release gate that is the autoscaler removing the node, 8.7 to 16.3 minutes per tenant swap: 21.7 minutes of `v2.0.1-rc.1`'s gate and 25.3 of `v2.0.0-rc.3`'s, 19% of each.

Measured on 2026-11-18 from the dogfood project's Cloud Logging, for all four swaps of gate runs 44236535751 and 44043297649.
Each `TeardownIncomplete` event names the previous leg's own worker: `still present: NetworkPolicy dogfood-e2e-workload (held for worker pods runner-ci-e2e-…)`.
In rc.1's first swap the job ended at 05:06:47Z and the gateway was deleted at 05:10:15Z.
The RunnerSet counted no active or pending job, so `reconcileDelete`'s Q547 drain gate passed and the AGC Deployment, RoleBinding and ServiceAccount went in the same pass.
The audit log shows the AGC never deleted the pod, and its reaper would have at `completedPodTTL` (5 minutes by default), at about 05:11:49Z.
The Q1328 hold then kept the workload policy, because `boundWorkerPods` lists pod metadata and counts a finished pod like a running one.
The autoscaler marked the idle node at 05:17:04Z, `pod-garbage-collector` deleted the orphaned pod at 05:18:52Z, and the delete returned at 05:18:54Z.
The final teardown of rc.3 ran 5.4 minutes after its last job, past the TTL, and returned in 2 seconds.

**Unverified:** the pod's phase during the hold (the audit query returned no kubelet status writes), and the static-node case, where pod GC leaves a terminated pod alone until the cluster passes its terminated-pod threshold, so the hold may be indefinite.
Reproduce on kind first: delete a gateway within 5 minutes of a job ending and read the held pod's phase.

Two fixes fit the design; the measured phase decides which is smaller:

- extend the Q547 drain gate to wait until every bound worker pod carries a deletion timestamp, so the AGC issues its `gateway_deleted` deletes before the GMC removes it, which is what `docs/design/04-operational-flows.md` already describes;
- or let the Q1328 hold skip pods in `Succeeded` or `Failed`, which run no container and so need no egress restriction.

**Done when** deleting a gateway seconds after its last job finishes completes without waiting for the pod's node, with envtest cases for a finished pod, a running pod (still held), and the no-AGC 90-second deadline; and `docs/design/04-operational-flows.md` and `docs/operations/troubleshooting.md` describe the new hold.
The Dragonfly gate leg empties the e2e pool before its dispatch (Q1363), so a fast swap leaves it a warm node only when that empty fails, which the gate logs as `e2e-pool-empty: warning:`.
Saving on the gate: 20–24 minutes per run (estimate, from the measured holds), with the system pool's two nodes and the gate's Actions minutes falling by the same.
