# Retire v1alpha1 from the design docs and controller comments

**The design docs.** [03-api-contracts.md § 3.1](../design/03-api-contracts.md#31-kubernetes-crd-schemas) is roughly 790 lines of `v1alpha1` Go schema, introduced as "authoritative for the running `v1alpha1` API", and [Appendix H](../design/appendix-h-v2-api-decomposition.md) is the v2 shape's source of truth.
Once neither v1 kind is served, § 3.1 describes nothing that runs and should point at Appendix H, or carry the v2 schema, rather than be edited in place.
`git grep -c "v1alpha1\|actions-gateway.github.com" -- docs/design` counts 35 lines in Appendix H, 11 in 03, 8 in 05-security and a handful elsewhere (measured 2026-11-01); some are history that should stay, so each wants reading rather than a sweep.
[07-test-plan.md](../design/07-test-plan.md) is the same shape: Q273 fixed the bullets that named a deleted test, and its AGC envtest bullets (reconciler end-to-end, Secret lifecycle, pod provisioning, failure recovery) still describe `RunnerGroup` suites that left with it; `git grep -c "RunnerGroup\|v1alpha1" -- docs/design/07-test-plan.md` counts 11 lines (measured 2026-11-02).
This is design docs only; the operator pages under `docs/operations/` are [Q1107](Q1107.md)'s.

**The GMC comments.** The GMC half of Q273 fixed the comments that stated something false once v1 left, and left the ones that only narrate the port: "Mirrors v1's buildHPA", "v1 parity (Q327)", "the v2 counterpart of v1's evalRunnerGroupHealth".
They name functions that no longer exist, so a reader cannot follow them, and the repo's comment standard is present tense with no history.
`git grep -n -i "v1's\|mirrors v1\|v1 parity\|counterpart of v1" -- cmd/gmc` finds them; `egressproxy_builder.go`, `actionsgateway_v2_builder.go` and `actionsgateway_v2_controller.go` hold most.

**The AGC comments.** The AGC half did the same: it fixed what turned false and left the narration, such as "the v2 counterpart of RunnerGroupReconciler" and "mirrors the v1 RunnerGroup watch".
`git grep -n -i -E "v1's|mirrors v1|v1 parity|counterpart of v1|v1 RunnerGroup|RunnerGroupReconciler|v2 twin" -- cmd/agc ':!cmd/agc/vendor' ':!cmd/agc/test/load'` finds 26 lines (measured 2026-11-02), 8 in `runnerset_controller.go`; `runner_shared.go` still introduces itself as machinery shared by the two kinds.
