# Act on the limit a killed runner's condition names

Blocked on [Q1403](Q1403.md)'s PRs 2 and 6, which write the condition and give the AGC an edge to see it by.

PR 7 of Q1403's design: a setting, off by default, through the GMC; a wait for the condition; text by reason; and no raise and no re-run where the limit that ran out was above the container.
Until it lands the AGC raises memory after a kill it made no difference to, beside a record that says so.
It rides 2.1 where it is ready and does not gate it, which is why it is a row of its own.

The wait ends on the counters' five reasons and on nothing else: a reason from the kernel log alone ([Q1407](Q1407.md)), or one the AGC does not know, is no condition to it, since a forged line can be there before the counters' answer.
A test holds that: a `Log` condition alone, and an unrecognised one, leave the wait running.
The duplicate search flags Q1400, which is the text this replaces.
