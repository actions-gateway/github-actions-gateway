# The release-ladder gate cannot express a revived count of one

`back_word` matches the literal `**N of the original M are back.**`, so a page down to a single revived item can only say "One ... are back", and "original M" is pinned to the current section total even after an item leaves the accounting.
Closing Q564 put that ungrammatical sentence into `docs/plan/release-ladder.md`.
Accept a singular verb, or read the count without pinning the wording.
