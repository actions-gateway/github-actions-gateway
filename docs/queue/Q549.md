# `E2E_AGC_ScaleSetDrainedWorkerClaimAndRerunLandUnderChartRBAC` times out

**Event:** recurs on `main` after the fix.

Three failures on `main` or in the merge queue between 2026-08-26 and 2026-08-28, in two shapes, neither of them the mode the row's own escalation guessed at.
Attributed 2026-08-28 from the AGC logs the failing runs captured; the [triage table](../plan/q549-scaleset-rerun-flake.md) carries the timings.

**Neither previously-known mode fired, and neither cause the spec's failure text names is real.** The two line-299 failures ([32933225396](https://github.com/actions-gateway/github-actions-gateway/actions/runs/32933225396), [33185281929](https://github.com/actions-gateway/github-actions-gateway/actions/runs/33185281929)) carry **zero** AGC log lines naming the probe pod: no claim, no `Forbidden`, no `Conflict`, no `NotFound`.
So the chart role is not short a verb and the deletion-mark discriminator did not regress: the scan reached no verdict on the pod, so the claim patch was never attempted.
Why it reached none is measured for 32933225396 only, where the pod's whole window falls in an 8-second reconcile gap; on 33185281929 three reconciles completed inside the interval and nothing here says why they produced nothing.
The line-264 failure ([32934571793](https://github.com/actions-gateway/github-actions-gateway/actions/runs/32934571793)) is the opposite: the AGC claimed the disruption and called rerun-failed-jobs, and the spec failed anyway, on `Expect(seq).NotTo(BeEmpty())` over a sampler its own comment calls diagnostics.

Mitigated by two changes.
The sampler is no longer asserted on; an empty sequence is a fact about a `kubectl`-per-sample sampler racing a fast teardown, not about the AGC.
And the scan now logs the terminating workers it judged and declined, which gives the spec a third "this attempt proves nothing" discriminator beside a replaced control plane and a lost claim: no verdict at all means the claim patch was never attempted, so the chart role was never exercised, and the attempt re-stages under the same `maxAttempts` budget.

Reaching `Fail` now means the AGC judged the pod, so the two causes the failure text names are the two that remain.
The product gap underneath is [Q1029](Q1029.md), which this row does not close: a drain's recovery is reachable only if a reconcile *begins* inside the pod's teardown window.
Residual: a regression in the scan's own List selector still presents as silence and would re-stage rather than fail.
Soak clock runs from the fix; blast radius is both e2e lanes, which are merge-group-only ([Q675](../development/testing.md#path-gated-workflows-verify-the-heavy-gates-actually-ran)).
