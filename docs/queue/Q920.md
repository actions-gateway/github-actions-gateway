# `gate-list.sh` never checks `DOCS_GATES` against its subset claim

`DOCS_GATES` says in its own comment that every entry is also in `CHECK_FAST_GATES`, "so like QUEUE_GATES this is a strict subset of `make check` and never a second opinion".
It is not: `release-notes-check` is in `DOCS_GATES` and in no other list (measured 2026-08-18 against `mk/gate-lists.mk`), so `make docs-gates` runs a gate `make check` does not.
`gate-list.sh` takes `--fast`, `--heavy`, `--queue` and `--suites`; its rules 4 and 7 hold `QUEUE_GATES` to that same claim in both directions, and nothing passes `DOCS_GATES` in at all.
Add a `--docs` argument and give it rules 4 and 7's treatment, then settle whether `release-notes-check` joins `CHECK_FAST_GATES` or leaves `DOCS_GATES`.
