# Two rows on one defect: a missed match and an ignored advisory

Q922, Q924 and Q987 all described one defect, docs naming the deleted `lint-backlog.sh` as live.
Three rows stood together for twelve days, two sessions picked different ones, and both fixes reached review before either noticed the other ([#1812](https://github.com/actions-gateway/github-actions-gateway/pull/1812) and [#1813](https://github.com/actions-gateway/github-actions-gateway/pull/1813)).

Replayed 2026-08-30 with `find-duplicate-rows.sh --store` against each store as it stood at filing time, so these are the verdicts the filing sessions actually saw.
The matcher did not simply miss them, and the two failures are different:

- **Q922 was filed with Q924 already present, and the matcher surfaced [Q830](Q830.md) at 0.43 instead.** Q830 is a false positive; Q924 scored below the floor and never appeared.
  Q922's author then did exactly what the advisory asks and wrote "Distinct from Q830", so the process ran correctly on a wrong signal.
  The two titles share **zero** content words and name different targets, so neither of the matcher's signals can reach the pair.
  They describe one defect at different altitudes: the class, and one file's instance of it.
- **Q987 was filed with Q922 present, and the matcher did flag it, at 0.50.** Q987 shipped citing Q922 zero times.
  The advisory says "If yours is genuinely separate, say so in its Notes"; nothing enforces that, and nothing recorded that the warning had been read and dismissed.

So one half is a recall gap the current signals cannot close, and the other is an advisory with no trace.
The second looks cheaper to fix and is the one that actually cost a duplicated session, since Q987 is where the third row entered.

Worth considering, and none of it measured: have `make queue-id` require a line naming any candidate it printed, the way the advisory already asks in prose; or record the printed candidates on the row so a later groom can see what was dismissed.
A title-only matcher may be the wrong instrument for rows that agree on the *defect* and not the words -- `target:` frontmatter and body text are both unused by it today.

Filed from the session that hit the collision; the measurement is in [#1812](https://github.com/actions-gateway/github-actions-gateway/pull/1812).
