# Scope running the Kata and DinD gate legs concurrently on dogfood

**Once the tenant-swap hold ([Q1362](Q1362.md)) is gone, the e2e jobs themselves pace the release gate, and running two legs at once is the only change left that takes a whole leg off its wall clock.** The DinD leg would take about 20 minutes once Q1362 lands (estimate, from its measured 17m23s and 19m31s jobs plus a fast swap).

On one cluster, not across projects: that is [Q1161](Q1161.md), deferred, which expects to cost more.
Here Kata and DinD would each need their own tenant namespace and RunnerSet, since today they are two overlays of one.
The e2e pool already allows two nodes (the autoscaler's `max: 2`), e2e node-hours should stay about the same, and system-pool hours should fall.
Dragonfly stays serial: its mirror back end and node client are cluster-wide.

**Measure:** each question below is a reading on dogfood or a read of the workflows, not a maintainer decision.

**The scoping pass should answer:**

- the project CPU quota headroom for two e2e workers beside the routed CI traffic, the contention Q631 exists for;
- how two dispatches of `e2e-test.yml` avoid its per-ref concurrency group, which holds one run and one pending slot;
- whether the Kata leg's mirror census stays complete with a second tenant's clients on the same mirrors;
- whether sizing and capacity, which read the Kata tenant after its leg, are disturbed by a concurrent DinD tenant.

**Done when** those are measured and the row either becomes an implementation item with a cost reading or is closed with the blocker named.
