# Tell a kill's fail-fast siblings from a timed-out job

The jobs list spells `cancelled` a leg its fail-fast matrix cancelled, a job past its `timeout-minutes` and a job a concurrency group cancelled, so the out-of-memory retry (Q1319) treats all of them alike: as another failed job, so a killed leg is re-run alone.
Counting none of them would re-run the siblings with it, and a timeout too.

The check run's annotations tell them apart (the readings), so the release could re-run the siblings and never a timeout.
That needs `Checks: read`, which the App has only where the optional `Checks: Read and write` is granted, one call for each cancelled job, and a wording that is GitHub's to change; without any of them it falls back to the rule above.

It settles at most a run whose only other failed jobs are cancelled: 12 of the 63 fail-fast runs read, since an aggregate that went red beside the leg is another failure whatever `cancelled` means.
Two readings come first: a lost runner's leg cancelling its siblings, and `rerun-failed-jobs` re-running a cancelled sibling.
Q1374 asks the same annotation of flaky-job detection, on its hot path.
