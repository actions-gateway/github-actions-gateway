# Tell a kill's fail-fast siblings from a timed-out job

The jobs list spells `cancelled` a leg its fail-fast matrix cancelled, a job past its `timeout-minutes` and a job a concurrency group cancelled, so the out-of-memory retry (Q1319) treats all of them alike: none is another failed job, so a killed leg's siblings are re-run with it where nothing else failed, and so is a timeout.

The check run's annotations tell them apart (the readings), so the release could re-run the siblings and never a timeout.
That needs `Checks: read`, which the App has only where the optional `Checks: Read and write` is granted, one call for each cancelled job, and a wording that is GitHub's to change; without any of them it keeps the rule above.

It stops at most the re-run of a timeout or a concurrency cancel beside a kill where nothing else failed: 1 and 3 of the 594 public failed runs read hold one, and 10 and 0 of this repository's 1,000.
Two readings come first: a lost runner's leg cancelling its siblings, and `rerun-failed-jobs` re-running a cancelled sibling.
Q1374 asks the same annotation of flaky-job detection, on its hot path.
