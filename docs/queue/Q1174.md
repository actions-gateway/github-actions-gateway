# A replayed scale-set provision can hand its pod a Secret being deleted

Since Q1154 a scale-set worker's JIT-config Secret is owned by its pod alone, so the garbage collector deletes the Secret after the pod goes.
Suppose a provision for the same job is replayed in the window between the pod's deletion and the collector's delete of the Secret (a re-created session replays its assignments from cursor 0).
`ProvisionScaleSetWorker` names both objects from the job ID, so the replay finds the old Secret, treats its `AlreadyExists` as success, creates a new pod, and patches the Secret's owner to that pod.
If the collector has already decided to delete the Secret, the new pod is left `Pending` on a Secret that no longer exists.

Reasoned from code by the Q1154 reviewer; not reproduced, and whether the collector re-checks owners after the patch is unmeasured.
It needs a replay of an assignment whose worker was deleted moments before, which the listener's held-message bookkeeping (Q583) makes rare but does not exclude.

**A likely fix**: on the `AlreadyExists` path, read the Secret and treat one with a `deletionTimestamp`, or one owned by a Pod that no longer exists, as absent: wait for it to go and re-create it.
A `Pending` worker on a missing Secret falls to the RunnerSet's `spec.pendingPodDeadline` reap, so the cost should be bounded by one worker's deadline rather than a stuck slot; that bound is read from the reaper, not exercised for this case.

**Reach.** Any scale-set tenant whose session is re-created while a worker is being deleted: a replayed job's worker waits out its `Pending` deadline instead of starting.
