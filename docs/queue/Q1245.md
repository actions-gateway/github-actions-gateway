# Alert on a misclassified retry class and gauge a paused breaker

Q1183 shipped the out-of-memory class's breaker as a `breaker_open` withhold plus one `AutoRetryPaused` Event, and left out three pieces [the design](../plan/q555-automatic-recovery-design.md#the-breaker-r11) names.
A `PrometheusRule` alert when a cause's failed share of `actions_gateway_eviction_rerun_outcomes_total` over 7 days passes 50%: R14's reading of a misclassified class, which the per-job `same_class_again` check cannot see across jobs.
A gauge, `actions_gateway_auto_retry_paused{namespace, runner_group, cause}`, reading 1 while a breaker is open, so a dashboard shows the pause rather than a rate of withholds.
And R16's cost split: an `actions-gateway.com/rerun-cause` annotation on a re-run's worker pods so [cost attribution](../operations/cost-attribution.md) can attribute re-run compute by cause.
Each new series needs the `deploy/monitoring/` artifacts per `docs/development/doc-update-matrix.md`.
