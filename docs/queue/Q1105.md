# The scripts-test job's timeout evicts a PR from the merge queue

**Event:** the `scripts-test` or `shellcheck` job of `unit-test.yml` concludes `cancelled` with every step green, or a PR leaves the merge queue because `unit-test-gate` read a gating job as `cancelled`.

Both mitigations have shipped and neither is proven to close the trigger, which is why this parks rather than retires.

**What shipped.** The cap went to 10 minutes (#1921) and `scripts-test` split into its own job, which is what cleared the eviction: 86s of work left in `shellcheck` against its 5-minute cap and ~253s in the new job against 10.
Then the CI fan-out was capped at four times the runner's vCPUs, taking a 4-vCPU runner from 130 concurrent suites to 16.

**What the cap is not evidence for.** It cuts per-suite wall-time inflation and is **not** shown to cut the job's wall time; those are different quantities.
Measured 2026-09-16 on an 18-core M5 Max, CPU-seconds is flat across every fan-out width from 9 to 130 (528-582s), so oversubscription wastes no CPU at that ratio, and the two widest runs finished in identical wall time.
No local reading ranks the caps at all: within-cap variance swamps the between-cap difference (cap 18 ran 106s and 182s on one tree, cap 0 ran 78s and 151s) as ambient load climbed 30 to 112 across the sweep.
The 4x multiple is the largest oversubscription the repo has evidence is harmless, not a measured optimum.

**The floor the cap cannot reach** is [Q1117](Q1117.md): `check-roadmap-test` costs 74-88s at every width, and a fan-out's wall time is its slowest member.

**The budget is not at risk today and the margin is not structural.** Measured 2026-09-16 over the last nine `unit-test.yml` runs carrying the job: `scripts-test` ran 153-210s on `ubuntu-latest` against a 600s cap, plus one 314s failure on the scaleset lane ([Q1116](Q1116.md), a different defect).
The suite count grows one per new gate (119 at Q822, 128 on 2026-09-13, 130 on 2026-09-16), so a budget sized to today expires.

Revive with the per-label block from the failing run: under a capped fan-out the suites no longer all stretch to the step's duration, so the slowest ones are legible and name what to shorten.
[Q966](Q966.md) owns the local throttle number, which this did not change; [Q822](Q822.md) owns the driver-build race.
