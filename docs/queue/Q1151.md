# AGC reaps a worker mid-job as orphaned_running after a sibling job ends

On 2026-09-30 the `gag-dogfood` tenant (`agc:v1.8.0-rc.2`, worker `dogfood-runner:2.337.0`) deleted two workers while their jobs were still running, each as `orphaned_running`, which is meant only for a pod still `Running` five minutes after GitHub reported its job finished.
GitHub reported both jobs as running until after the delete, and each job then failed with `The runner has received a shutdown signal`.

| Job (run [36751334381](https://github.com/actions-gateway/github-actions-gateway/actions/runs/36751334381), [36751338479](https://github.com/actions-gateway/github-actions-gateway/actions/runs/36751338479)) | Runner | GitHub: started → completed | Reaped `orphaned_running` | Reap − 5m |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `lint` | `gag-ci-scaleset-ca1cae15-…` | 17:29:20 → 17:35:15Z | 17:35:12Z | 17:30:12Z |
| `integration-test (agc)` | `gag-ci-scaleset-7d6b7dca-…` | 17:32:04 → 17:39:30Z | 17:39:27Z | 17:34:27Z |

The last column is when the AGC must have stamped each job complete, and each lines up with a *different* job on the same RunnerSet actually completing: `actionlint` and `uses-pinned` at 17:30:11Z, `shellcheck` at 17:34:27Z.
Not every completion does it: `cosign-pin` completed at 17:32:38Z and `integration-test (gmc)` ran past 17:37:38Z and passed.
The pod's `WorkerPodOrphanedRunning` event reads `was still Running 5m0s after its job completed`, and a `FailedMount` for its `job-payload` secret (`job-ss-<jobID>-<hash>" not found`) preceded the reap by 50 s, so the AGC had also cleaned up that job's payload secret.

**Mechanism unverified.** The measured shape is a completion for one job being recorded against another job's worker; how is not established.
Three spot preemptions of `workers` nodes (GCE `compute.instances.preempted`, 17:31:03-10Z) killed three other jobs in the same burst and are a separate cause, but they fall between the two stamps, so they are not obviously the trigger.
The AGC logs nothing at info level for the reaped job IDs before the reap itself.

**Reach.** Any tenant on this build loses a running job whenever this fires, and the operator sees an ordinary `orphaned_running` reap.
The runbook reads that as a worker that never got its job, which is the opposite of what happened here.
