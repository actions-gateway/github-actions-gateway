# Nothing gates a plan doc's Status header against the repo

Twice in one session a plan's Status line claimed a shipped phase: `Phase 1 in progress` after two merged, `4 to 6 not started` with 4 in review.
Caught by reading, not a gate.
Half of this is now gated: Q889 rekeyed `plan-index-check` invariant 1 onto the *index row's* Status cell, which caught two rows claiming open work after every phase had shipped.
The plan doc's own Status header has no gate at all, and that is the half left.
A plan naming merged PRs while claiming a phase is open is gateable.
