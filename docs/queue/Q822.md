# Three unrelated suites fail under concurrent load and pass on rerun

Measured 2026-08-12 under 8 workers: `provisioner` eviction-window and `controller` 2m timeout failed in one window, all green on rerun.
A third suite failed with them and has since been deleted with the piped-gate hook.

**The contention half is now proven and the row's scope is not.** Measured 2026-08-24: `make scripts-test` runs 93 distinct suite scripts at once, peaking at 221 processes, against the 96 suites the gate list names.
`run-parallel.sh` launches every command with `&` and waits, with no cap anywhere in it, and no `scripts/ci/` script takes `serialize_heavy_build`.
So a session running the fast gates fans out ~93 ways on 18 cores while a sibling's locked heavy phase gets no protection from it.
[The measurement and what it does not show](../development/testing.md#the-fast-gates-fan-out-past-the-heavy-build-semaphore).

Two corrections before this is implemented as written.
The `controller` 2m timeout is the budget [Q823](Q823.md) raised to 5m, so one of the two remaining symptoms is already addressed and only `provisioner` is still observable here.
And the fan-out is measured while its link to either failure is not: nothing here reproduces a suite failing because of it.

Capping the fan-out is a machine-wide throughput decision of the same kind as [Q966](Q966.md) and wants the same hands: `run-parallel.sh` also backs `docs-gates` and `queue-gates`, where 19 short commands at once is the behaviour you want.
