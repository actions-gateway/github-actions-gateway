# Reach the install doc's two kubelet-dependent blocks

`TestGettingStarted_Executable` walks nine of `docs/getting-started.md`'s fourteen fenced blocks against the GMC envtest apiserver, and `make getting-started-render-check` renders the tenth.
Four are declared `mode=skip` in the page itself, each with its reason, and two of those are worth reaching: `rotate-rollout-status` and `rotate-verify-logs`, the credential-rotation steps that run `kubectl rollout status` and `kubectl logs` against the AGC Deployment.
There is no kubelet in envtest, so nothing rolls and no pod logs: a venue limit rather than anything wrong with the page.

The obvious venue is a kind cluster, and the reason this is `M` rather than `S` is that a kind cluster alone does not settle it: block `github-app-secret` supplies a placeholder private key, so the AGC never authenticates and the rollout the doc tells the operator to wait for never completes.
Reaching these two means substituting `test/fakegithub` for the doc's `githubURL`, and that substitution is the question to answer before building anything: a walk over text the gate has rewritten is weaker evidence than the doc's own bytes, and it is not obvious the two blocks are worth buying at that price.
Settle that first, then pick the venue.
Measured 2026-08-21: the e2e lane runs a median 14 min (n=11, 2.9-23.8) and is merge-group-only (Q675), against about a second for the envtest walk on a job already running.

The other two skips stay skipped and need no item: `crds-v2-release-asset` names a release asset that exists only after a tag, and `crds-v2-oci-render` pulls a remote OCI chart.
