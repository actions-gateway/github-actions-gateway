# A fourth GMC integration test breaches its 15s wait, cause unmeasured

**Event:** any 15 s `require.Eventually` in the GMC integration suite breaches on `main`.
This subsumes [Q797](Q797.md)'s narrower trigger, so that row delegates to this one: a failure of its three fires this Event, and both rows escalate together.
This row owns the class signal and the measurement.

Sighted once, 2026-08-27 07:52 PDT, on a local `make test-integration`: `TestV2_ActionsGateway_ProvisionsAGCControlPlane` failed after 15.52 s at line 151 of [v2_actionsgateway_test.go](../../cmd/gmc/internal/controller/integration/v2_actionsgateway_test.go), `Condition never satisfied`, on the wait for the AGC Deployment to appear.
The observing session's diff touched no `cmd/gmc` file, and `cmd/agc` is a separately compiled module, so for this suite the tree it ran was `origin/main`.

**Load is present and is not sufficient**, which is the finding.
Measured 2026-08-27 on the same machine at `d36b09c`, every run `-race` through `make -C cmd/gmc test-integration`:

| Condition | Load avg | Package | The test |
|---|---|---|---|
| Isolated, 6 runs | 7 to 14 | n/a | 1.19 to 1.39 s |
| Full suite, 2 runs | 2 to 6 | 231.5 s, 231.7 s | 1.35 s, 1.36 s |
| Full suite under 18 unniced CPU burners plus disk churn, 3 runs | 36 to 39 | 285.1 to 286.8 s | 1.49 to 1.89 s |
| The sighting | ~20.5 | 278.2 s | failed at 15.52 s |

The sighting's 278.2 s sits on the loaded curve, at 1.20x the quiet baseline where deliberate load gives 1.23x, so that session's window really was slow and the suite slowdown reproduces.
The failure does not.
At nearly double the sighting's load average the assertion costs 1.89 s, and breaching 15 s needs 11x.
Whatever held that one reconcile past 15 s is an outlier rather than a proportional slowdown, and nothing measured here names it.

**The clean runs barely bound the rate.** Zero failures in five in-suite runs puts the 95 % upper bound on the per-run rate near 60 % by the rule of three, so the absent repro is close to no evidence of rarity.

The window is not tight by the suite's own standard: 15 s is its modal `Eventually` budget and the measured cost leaves 11x headroom, so raising the number moves the threshold instead of fixing anything.
The test client is `client.New` (uncached), so the delay sits on the reconciler side: manager cache sync, the reconcile itself, or a starved envtest apiserver.
One candidate, **unverified**: the suite runs under `nice -n 10 taskpolicy -d throttle` (`scripts/agent/local-throttle.sh`), which deprioritises its etcd on CPU *and* disk while unniced peer sessions compete at normal priority.

Same class as [Q797](Q797.md), which holds the first sighting (three `TestGMC_TenantProvisioning_*` at the same 15 s, 2026-08-11) and whose own Event has not fired.
A sweep of 1,432 local session transcripts finds no second failure of its three, and `integration-test` on `main` is 23/25 over its last 25 runs with neither red being one of them.
[Q968](Q968.md) is the same shape in `cmd/agc`, and its own investigation traced that breach to a shutdown defect rather than to load ([#1754](https://github.com/actions-gateway/github-actions-gateway/pull/1754), merged 2026-08-27 as `17de0762f`).
It refuted its load reading the way this row refutes its own, and then found a product cause underneath, which is the nearest evidence that a breach in this family can be a defect rather than an environment.
[Q1008](Q1008.md) is the sighting that *did* land on `main`, and its cause turned out to be a test defect rather than load, which is the second row in this family to end that way.
This row owns the load measurement, so cite it by ID rather than restating the figures.
