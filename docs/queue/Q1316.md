# Retry a job whose Docker-in-Docker sidecar crashed under it

A job that fails because its Docker-in-Docker sidecar crashed is a platform failure, and today it needs a manual re-run.
The design's third retry class recognises it from the worker snapshot, where the `dockerd` sidecar's restart count rose while the job ran, and it clears with a fresh pod.

The discriminator is the open part, and it may not exist.
[The readings](../plan/q555-automatic-recovery-design.md#readings-for-the-dockerd-sidecar-class-q1316), taken on 2026-11-24 on one local node image, settle these points:

- A job that fills the sidecar's disk does not crash `dockerd`; the next `docker run` fails and GAG sees nothing.
- An out-of-memory crash is the job's own under `kata-dind`, and reads the same whether the job or the node caused it under `privileged-dind`.
- The shapes no job causes by accident have no known platform cause, on the shipped templates, that leaves a serving runner alive; a daemon that fails its first start is the exception, and [Q1397](Q1397.md)'s startup probe prevents it.
- The usage sampler can show the sidecar reached its limit and cannot show that it did not.

What is left:

- **Reproduce (R12)** a platform-caused crash on dogfood, and the nearest tenant-caused one, then find a fact GAG owns that separates them (R9 rules out reading job logs).
  The first candidate is the kernel's `oom` and `oom_kill` counters: on one local kernel the pod's `oom` is 0 for a kill from above it, and `kubepods`' own tells a fill by pods from one by a job's containers outside it ([what is left open](../plan/q555-automatic-recovery-design.md#what-is-left-open-q1316)).
  No path carries them to the AGC, and whether a node kill reaches the sidecar before the runner is unread.
- **If none separates them**, the class is not buildable as designed; say so in [the design](../plan/q555-automatic-recovery-design.md#classes-after-the-first) and punt this row rather than shipping a class that retries tenant failures.

Gated on 2.1 as part of its headline ([release-2.1.md](../plan/release-2.1.md)).
