# Retry a job whose Docker-in-Docker sidecar crashed under it

A job that fails because its Docker-in-Docker sidecar crashed is a platform failure, and today it needs a manual re-run.
The design's third retry class recognises it from the worker snapshot, where the `dockerd` sidecar's restart count rose while the job ran, and it clears with a fresh pod.

The discriminator is the open part, and it may not exist.
[The readings](../plan/q555-automatic-recovery-design.md#readings-for-the-dockerd-sidecar-class-q1316), taken on 2026-11-24 on one local node image, settle these points:

- A job that fills the sidecar's disk does not crash `dockerd`; the next `docker run` fails and GAG sees nothing.
- An out-of-memory crash is the job's own under `kata-dind`, and reads the same whether the job or the node caused it under `privileged-dind`.
- The shapes no job causes by accident have no known platform cause, on the shipped templates, that leaves a serving runner alive; a daemon that fails its first start is the exception, and the templates' startup probe prevents it.
- The usage sampler can show the sidecar reached its limit and cannot show that it did not.

[The dogfood runs](../plan/q1316-dogfood-reproduction.md) of 2026-11-24 and 25 read the rest on GKE:

- In the second run, a neighbour that fills the node to `kubepods`' limit killed the runner, not the sidecar, in four fills of four, two at the shipped scores and two with the daemon's score written to the runner's (the write was not read back).
- So no platform cause that leaves the runner alive to fail the job has been found, and the counter a killed sidecar's kill would raise is unread, because none died.
- A job's kill under Kata leaves the sidecar `OOMKilled`, 143, which is the status Q1395's rule is keyed on; the AGC that ran there, `v2.0.1-rc.2`, predates that rule, so the rule was not run.
- A node-wide kill was not made.
  Its order is computed, with the runner's lead larger than at `kubepods`' limit.

So the class is not buildable as designed at the shipped requests, and [the design](../plan/q555-automatic-recovery-design.md#classes-after-the-first) says so: nothing read kills the sidecar and leaves the runner alive to fail the job, which a class needs, and a class that fired anyway would retry jobs that failed for their own reason.
What it costs is that a sidecar that dies first stays a manual re-run, and nothing says how often that is.

**Event:** a platform cause that kills the sidecar with its runner alive is on record.
The likely carriers are detection's record of a job whose `dockerd` restarted with `Error` and which passed when re-run, a template that requests 3Gi or more for its runner beside a daemon that outgrows it, and a node-wide kill, whose order is computed and not read.

Not a 2.1 gate, for the reasons above ([release-2.1.md](../plan/release-2.1.md#explicitly-out-of-scope)).
