# Retry a job whose Docker-in-Docker sidecar crashed under it

A job that fails because its Docker-in-Docker sidecar crashed is a platform failure, and today it needs a manual re-run.
The design's third retry class recognises it from the worker snapshot, where the `dockerd` sidecar's restart count rose while the job ran, and it clears with a fresh pod.

The discriminator is the open part, and it may not exist: a tenant job that fills the sidecar's disk or exhausts its memory also crashes `dockerd`, and re-running that masks real breakage.
So the row is a reproduction before it is a build:

- **Reproduce (R12)** a platform-caused crash on dogfood, and the nearest tenant-caused one (disk filled by the job's own images or layers), then find a fact GAG owns that separates them (R9 rules out reading job logs).
- **If none separates them**, the class is not buildable as designed; say so in [the design](../plan/q555-automatic-recovery-design.md#classes-after-the-first) and punt this row from 2.1 rather than shipping a class that retries tenant failures.

Gated on 2.1 as part of its headline ([release-2.1.md](../plan/release-2.1.md)); of the 2.1 rows it is the one most likely to be punted.
