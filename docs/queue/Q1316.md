# Retry a job whose Docker-in-Docker sidecar crashed under it

**Event:** detection's records show a failed job whose `dockerd` sidecar restarted with reason `Error`, and the job passed when somebody re-ran it, on more than one run.
Read the record from the AGC's `scale-set job failed` line (its `context` holds each container's reason, exit code and restart count) or from the `scaleset-failures-<runnerset>` ConfigMap while the record is open.

The design's third retry class recognises a platform crash of `dockerd` from its worker's snapshot and re-runs the job on a fresh pod.
It is not buildable from what the snapshot holds: an out-of-memory crash reads the same whether the job or the platform caused it, and the shapes no job causes by accident have no known platform cause ([why, with the readings](../plan/q555-automatic-recovery-design.md#why-the-dockerd-sidecar-class-is-not-built-q1316)).
Whoever revives it also needs the crash's time past an AGC restart, when the runner started, and any crash before the last, none of which the record holds.
