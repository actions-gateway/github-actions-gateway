# The eviction arm re-runs a job evicted for its own storage limit

The `eviction` recovery cause keys on pod `reason: Evicted` alone (`eviction_scaleset.go`, and `provisioner.go` on classic), and the kubelet sets that reason both for node pressure and for a pod that exceeds its own `ephemeral-storage` limit.
The live eviction-recovery e2e (`github_scaleset_test.go`, `scaleSetLiveManifest`) evicts its worker exactly the second way and expects the re-run, so a job that outgrows its own limit is re-run up to `maxEvictionRetries` (default 2) and will likely fail the same way each time.
Troubleshooting's boundary table documents the arm as "Kubelet eviction (node pressure)", which is narrower than the code.
Both readings are from source, 2026-10-10; nothing has measured what `status.message` the kubelet writes for each shape.
First measure the two messages on kind, then decide: exclude the limit shape (the storage analogue of `OOMKilled`, which Q555's draft rules out of retry), or keep it and say so in the boundary table.
Excluding it also needs another eviction lever for that e2e spec.
