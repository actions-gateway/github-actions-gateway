# Advisory conditions go stale on the fail-closed reconcile path

`ActionsGatewayV2Reconciler.Reconcile` returns at `setNotReady(...)` for a missing App `Secret`, `ProxyNotFound`, `ProxyShareNotGranted` and the `githubCABundleRef` failures, all of them before `updateStatus` runs.
`setNotReady` writes the named condition and `Ready` and nothing else, so every advisory condition `updateStatus` owns keeps the value it last held: `EgressUnattributed`, `AGCAutoscalingUnavailable`, `ScaleSetNameCollision`, `RunnerSetsDegraded` and `EgressAuditUnattributed`, plus `status.proxyMode`.

**Measured 2026-09-07** on a fake-client reconcile (throwaway test, not committed): reconcile a gateway whose `defaultProxyRef` pool logs `ConnectionsWithSource` and whose own `auditLogging` is `WorkerAddresses`, then delete the `EgressProxy` and reconcile again.
`Ready` correctly becomes `False`/`ProxyNotFound`, and beside it the egress-audit condition stays on its joined reason and `EgressUnattributed` stays `False`/`ProxiedEgress`.
(Measured before #1870 inverted that condition's polarity; the staleness is the reconcile path's and is unaffected by which way it reads.)
A gateway with no proxy therefore reports its egress as proxied and its audit records as joinable.

**The AGC's `RunnerSet` reconciler has the same shape.** On an unresolved reference it returns after `setReadyCondition`, and it clears the reap-blocking-sidecar and worker-capacity conditions but not `EgressUnattributed` or `EgressAuditUnattributed`, so both keep their last value, and so does `actions_gateway_runnerset_egress_audit_unattributed`, which is collected from the condition.
**Measured 2026-10-09** on a fake-client reconcile (throwaway test, not committed): a set whose own `proxyRef` pool logs `ConnectionsWithSource` under a gateway logging `WorkerAddresses` reconciled to `EgressAuditUnattributed=False`/`EgressAuditJoined`; after deleting that `EgressProxy` and reconciling again, `Ready` became `False`/`ProxyDeleted` while the condition stayed `False`/`EgressAuditJoined` and the gauge stayed `0`/`EgressAuditJoined`.
Whichever shape this row picks applies to both reconcilers, and the per-set gauge is the one [Q1070](Q1070.md)'s panel keys on.

Reading this as a defect is the part that needs deciding, and it is not obvious.
The fail-closed path deliberately writes as little as possible, and a condition carries `observedGeneration` and `lastTransitionTime` precisely so a reader can tell a current status from one the reconciler has not revisited.
Against that: nothing forces a reader to check, a Prometheus gauge carries neither field, and `EgressAuditUnattributed` is the one whose stale `False` an audit panel would key on (Q1062, Q1070).

Three shapes, cheapest first:
- **Do nothing and document it**, in `docs/design/appendix-h-v2-api-decomposition.md` §H.7 where the uniform status contract lives, so the staleness is a stated property of every advisory condition rather than a surprise per condition.
- **Have `setNotReady` clear the advisory set** to a `Unknown`/`NotReconciled` state, which is honest and touches every consumer of those conditions including the `RunnerSetsDegraded` rollup.
- **Gate the gauges on `Ready`**, which is the smallest change and the least honest: it silences the metric on a gateway that may be fine except for one missing reference.

Found while reviewing #1870 (Q1062), whose first draft asserted the opposite mechanism in its own row.
