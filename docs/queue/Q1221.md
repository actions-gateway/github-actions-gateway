# Decide whether an untrusted job gets a narrower runner credential

Every credential a worker pod holds is readable by the job's own code, and nobody has listed them or asked whether an untrusted job should get fewer.
This is the second of three gaps [the multi-tenant goal](../plan/secure-multi-tenant-oss-ci.md#open-decisions-the-map-surfaced) surfaced.

**What is known.** On the scale-set tier each worker mounts a per-job Secret holding its runner's just-in-time (JIT) registration config, owned by the pod so it goes when the pod does ([05-security.md](../design/05-security.md), "A Runner Credential Outliving Its Worker").
That row bounds the credential's *lifetime*.
What the job's code can do with it while the job runs, such as registering a second runner or acquiring another job, is not written down anywhere, and neither is the classic tier's equivalent.
The workflow's own `GITHUB_TOKEN` is minted by GitHub from the workflow's `permissions:`, so its scope is the repository's decision and out of this row.

**Measure:** enumerate every credential a worker pod can read on both acquisition tiers (mounted Secrets, environment, and files the runner writes during the job), and for each, what it permits when used from inside the job before the job ends.
**Ask:** once that list exists, whether any item is worth narrowing for an untrusted class, and whether narrowing belongs per `RunnerSet` or to the class [Q1222](Q1222.md) defines.
