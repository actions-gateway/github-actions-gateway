# Re-run one job whose runner was killed, on dogfood

The out-of-memory retry re-runs the killed job alone where another job of its run also failed (`POST …/jobs/{id}/rerun`, Q1319).
No reading covers that call on a killed job: M6's single-job re-runs were all of jobs that ended on their own, and the one killed job it read was re-run by `rerun-failed-jobs`.

If GitHub refuses the call, that retry never lands, which would read as `actions_gateway_eviction_rerun_failures_total{cause="oom"}` rising and the job needing a manual re-run, after its budget slot was spent.
It accepts the call for a failed job and for a succeeded one, and lists a killed job as an ordinary `failure` with its runner's name, so a refusal is not expected.

Take it on the 2.1 candidate's dogfood validation or sooner: one run with a job the class retries and a job that fails on its own, on a `Binpack` or `Throughput` set with samples.
Record that the killed job alone ran again, that the failed sibling kept its first attempt's `started_at`, and that the run's conclusion stayed `failure`.
The same run answers a second question M6 left: what a later attempt's list shows for a killed job it did not re-run, which two killed jobs beside the failure show.
It needs a dogfood grant.
